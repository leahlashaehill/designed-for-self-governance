# Designed for Self-Governance

An interactive research dashboard on the International Food Biotechnology Council (IFBC) as a case study in industry-designed food safety oversight. It draws on internal industry records in the UCSF Industry Documents Library.

**[Open the dashboard](https://leahlashaehill.github.io/designed-for-self-governance/)** (after GitHub Pages is enabled)

## What's here

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The dashboard: one self-contained file, no dependencies |
| `source-documentation.md` | Every archival document (with UCSF IDs and links) and every quotation used |
| `implementation-notes.md` | How the timeline works, how to add events, quotes and documents, and known limitations |

## Evidence standards

- All 133 document identifiers were confirmed in the UCSF Industry Documents Library index.
- All 58 quotations were matched against each document's OCR text.
- Events are labeled Verified, Likely, Proposed (no record found), Industry claim, or External source.

The research does not claim that the IFBC caused FDA policy. Similarities between industry recommendations and FDA policy are labeled as similarities, not influence.
